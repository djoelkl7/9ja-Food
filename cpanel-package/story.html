The Naija Pot Story

Born from a passion for authentic Nigerian buka flavours, woodfire charcoal grills, and the warm, spirited camaraderie of Lagos dining.

HERITAGE

From Lagos Street Bukkas to Modern Culinary Elegance

At NAIJA POT KITCHEN & GRILL LTD, food is more than sustenance — it is a vibrant language of joy, family, culture, and celebration.

Growing up between the historic open markets of Lagos Island and the night-time charcoal suya pits of northern Nigeria, our culinary team learned that authentic Nigerian food cannot be rushed. It requires deep respect for slow caramelization, the pungent perfume of fermented locust beans (iru), hand-selected stockfish, and pure cold-pressed palm oil.

When we established Naija Pot in London, our goal was clear: recreate that unapologetic, full-bodied flavour profile without dilution or shortcuts. When our guests taste our Smoky Party Jollof, they are instantly transported to a Lagos wedding Saturday. When they break into our Pounded Yam with Royal Egusi, they taste home.
